The Ulysses pact

Ulysses wanted to hear the sirens' song without throwing himself toward it. Before drawing near, he had himself tied to the mast and ordered his crew not to untie him, whatever he said next. He knew that in that moment, he would no longer want the same thing. Pactulys takes up that pact: you decide with a clear head, and your computer keeps your word for you.

Why a lock that can't be lifted?

Ordinary blockers can be turned off in two clicks, at the very moment you'd want them to hold. Pactulys starts from the opposite principle: the decision is made with a clear head, and it holds through the moment of weakness.

How it works

  1. 1Install

    On your Windows PC

    Blocking starts as soon as it's installed: pornographic sites blocked, search filtered, private browsing closed. On ordinary sites, explicit images are blurred, with the analysis done on your PC.

  2. 2Commit

    Choose a length of time

    When you're ready, you set a lock: an hour, a week, longer. You can extend it, never shorten it.

  3. 3Hold

    Until the term ends

    While the lock runs, Pactulys defends itself: stopping it, uninstalling it and known workarounds are all refused. At the end, you're back in control.

Our commitments, in writing before you buy

  • A lock in progress always runs its full term.

    Neither the end of your subscription, nor a missed payment, nor a request to support will lift it.

  • No exit on request.

    We never lift a lock because someone asks us to. The only exceptions: a life-threatening emergency or a legal obligation, through a written and recorded procedure.

  • Your administrator access stays yours.

    A paper recovery key, which you entrust to someone close to you or keep in a safe, gives you back full access to your machine.

  • No surveillance.

    Our server knows only the time and the state of the lock. No visited site, no history and no image leaves your computer. Filtering happens on the device.

What Pactulys doesn't do

We would rather say this plainly than leave you to discover it:

  • it protects the Windows PC it is installed on, not your other devices;
  • a full reinstall of Windows, or booting into another system, gets around it;
  • it does not read the contents of encrypted files.

Its aim: to make getting around it long and deliberate, not a two-second reflex.

Frequently asked questions

What if I change my mind during the lock?

The lock runs to its term: that's the whole point. Choose a short length of time to begin with. You can always extend it, never shorten it.

What happens when the lock ends?

Blocking stays on, but you get all your rights back: you can turn it off, set a new lock or uninstall Pactulys.

What does your server see?

The time and the state of the lock, nothing else. It provides a reliable time, so that nobody can move the computer's clock forward to cut the lock short.

What if I stop paying?

A lock in progress continues until its term ends. Without an active subscription, you simply can't set a new one.

What about phones?

Not yet. We will choose the first mobile platform based on what people on the waitlist tell us.
